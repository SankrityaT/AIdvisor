How it works

From “I have no idea” to a full plan, in four steps.

Answer a few questions. Compass maps your major, weighs your goals, and hands you a semester-by-semester route — then stays with you when things change.

1

Tell Compass about you

Intended major, career goal, how much risk you want, and whether you'd rather learn deeply or protect your GPA.
